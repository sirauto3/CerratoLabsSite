Shape Chaser Privacy Policy

Effective date: To be finalized before App Store submission.

Overview

Shape Chaser is designed as an offline game. The app does not require an account, does not include third-party advertising, and is not designed to collect personal information.

Data Stored On Device

Shape Chaser may store gameplay settings and progress locally on the device, such as unlocked levels, completed levels, tutorial status, difficulty selection, and shape selection.

Data We Collect

Cerrato Labs does not receive gameplay progress, personal information, or device identifiers from Shape Chaser in the planned first release.

Third Parties

The planned first release does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or account services. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, crash, and transaction information under Apple's own policies.

Children's Privacy

Shape Chaser is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@cerratolabs.com.
